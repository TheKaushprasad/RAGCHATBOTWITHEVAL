How Queryva works

Five steps between your question and an answer you can check.

  1. Ingest

    Your files are split into 350-token chunks that keep their headings and page numbers, then embedded into a vector index.

  2. Search

    Semantic search pulls the 20 passages closest in meaning to your question, from your files only.

  3. Rerank

    A model reads the question and all 20 together, scores how well each one answers it, and keeps the best 5.

  4. Check relevance

    If nothing is close enough, Queryva answers “I don’t know” before an LLM is ever called.

  5. Answer with citations

    The answer is written only from those passages, and every claim links back to its source.

FAQ

What can I ask Queryva?

Anything covered by the documents you upload: PDFs, Word files, Markdown or text. Queryva answers only from your own files and cites the passage behind every claim. If the answer isn’t in them, it says so.

Which file types can I upload?

PDF, Word (.docx), Markdown and plain text, up to 4 MB each and 5 files in total. Scanned PDFs without a text layer aren’t supported, because there is no OCR.

Who can see the documents I upload?

Only you. Uploads are stored in your account, and Queryva only ever searches your own files, never anyone else’s. They stay until you remove them from My documents.

Do I need an account?

Yes, a free one: an email and a password. It keeps your chat history and uploads on any device. You can reset a forgotten password by email from the login page.

What happens when the answer isn’t in my documents?

Queryva says “I don’t know” instead of guessing. Clearly off-topic questions are caught before the LLM is called; for on-topic questions the documents don’t cover, the model is instructed to refuse rather than use outside knowledge.

Can Queryva still be wrong?

Yes. It can pick the wrong passage or give an incomplete answer, which is why every claim is cited so you can check it. On the 100-question golden dataset, 88% of answers were fully correct and none contained claims unsupported by the passages.

Which AI models does it use?

OpenAI text-embedding-3-small for search, and gpt-4.1-mini for reranking and answers. Google Gemini is supported as an alternative provider.
