# Plans and Billing

## Plans

Tidepool has three plans.

**Free** — up to 5 members per workspace, up to 3 pools, 1 GB of total storage, and 14 days of activity history. Integrations are limited to two at a time.

**Current** — $8 per member per month when billed annually, or $10 per member per month when billed monthly. Unlimited pools, 100 GB of storage per workspace, 1 year of activity history, and unlimited integrations. Includes guest access.

**Riptide** — $16 per member per month, billed annually only. Everything in Current plus SAML single sign-on, audit logs, unlimited activity history, data residency selection, and a 99.9% uptime SLA. Riptide requires a minimum of 10 seats.

Nonprofits and accredited schools get 50% off the Current plan. Apply from **Workspace settings → Billing → Discounts** with proof of status; approval usually takes 3 business days.

## Guests

Guests can see and comment on ripples in the pools they are invited to, but cannot create pools or see open pools they weren't invited to. On the Current and Riptide plans, each paid member allows 5 free guests. Guests beyond that allowance are billed as full members. Guests are not available on the Free plan.

## Trials

Every new team workspace can start a 21-day trial of the Riptide plan once. No credit card is required to start the trial. When the trial ends without a subscription, the workspace moves back to the Free plan. If the workspace exceeds Free limits at that point, existing pools stay readable but you cannot create new ripples until you remove pools or upgrade.

## Payment methods

We accept Visa, Mastercard, and American Express. Invoicing with bank transfer is available for annual Riptide subscriptions of 25 seats or more. We do not accept PayPal or cryptocurrency.

Invoices are emailed to the billing contact on the 1st of every month (monthly plans) or on the subscription anniversary (annual plans). Past invoices can be downloaded as PDFs from **Workspace settings → Billing → Invoices**.

## Adding and removing seats

Adding a member to a paid workspace is prorated for the remainder of the billing period. Removing a member on a monthly plan takes effect at the next billing date. On annual plans, removed seats are credited to your account balance, not refunded to your card.

## Refunds and cancellation

You can cancel at any time from **Workspace settings → Billing → Cancel subscription**. Monthly plans are not refunded for the current month. Annual plans cancelled within 30 days of purchase or renewal receive a full refund; after 30 days, no refund is issued, and the plan remains active until the end of the paid year.

After cancellation the workspace drops to the Free plan. Data is never deleted because of a downgrade.

## Failed payments

If a payment fails, we retry three times over 9 days and email the billing contact after each attempt. If all retries fail, the workspace becomes **read-only** until payment is updated. After 60 days in read-only state, the workspace is downgraded to Free.
